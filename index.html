Make a Southern Africa Birding Stats log

Turn your Southern Africa bird sightings into an interactive page with maps, a life list, target hotspots and charts of your birding over the years.

How it works

  1. Export your sightings as a CSV file from your birding app or website (for example Sasol eBird, the Roberts Bird app or BirdLasser).
  2. Click Choose File below and pick that CSV. You can pick more than one; sightings logged in both are counted once.
  3. Type a heading for your page and choose whether bird names open in Afrikaans or English. You can switch inside the report too.
  4. Wait for the button to say Make my report (the first load takes 10 to 20 seconds), then click it.
  5. Your report opens in a new tab. Use Download a copy if you want to keep it or send it to someone.

Your privacy: everything happens inside your own browser. Your file is never uploaded, and the only thing counted on this page is that a report was made.

Tips: use Chrome, Safari or Firefox on a computer with an internet connection. If the new tab doesn't open, allow pop-ups for this site or use the Open report link that appears.
